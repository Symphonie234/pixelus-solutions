Pixelus Deluxe Solutions

A fan-made solution archive for Pixelus Deluxe. Moves are counted the way the game counts them: every tile slid in or pulled out is one move.

Pixelus Deluxe is a game by Nuclide Games, published by PopCap Games. This site is unofficial and not affiliated with either. The character sprites are original fan art.

Game facts from Wikipedia, MobyGames, RAWG and Softpedia.
